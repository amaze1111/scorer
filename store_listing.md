# Google Play Store Listing

## App name (max 30 characters)

Scorer: Game Score Keeper

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Keep score of your card games and show the live scoreboard on your TV. Offline.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Scorer keeps score of your table games, so nobody needs pen and paper. Enter points on your phone, see totals update live, and show the scoreboard on your TV so everyone at the table can see it.

Rummy scoring is available now. Support for more games is planned.

LIVE SCORING
• Enter points as you play. Totals update straight away.
• Scorer applies the scoring rules of the game for you.
• Every round adds up into a running total for each player.

TARGET SCORE AND WINNER
• Set a target score before the game starts.
• Scorer ends the game when a player reaches the target and shows the winner.
• A tie for first place? Scorer tells you to play another round.

SHOW THE SCOREBOARD ON YOUR TV (OPTIONAL)
• Scorer runs on your phone. A TV is not required.
• Cast the scoreboard to a Chromecast or Cast-enabled TV. Nothing to install on the TV.
• Have an Android TV or Google TV? You can also send the app from your phone to the TV over Wi-Fi for a direct link.
• Big, clear numbers that everyone can read from the sofa.

FIX MISTAKES EASILY
• Undo the last entry.
• Reopen the last round.
• Tap any score of a finished round to edit it.

PAST GAMES
• Finished games are saved with date, rounds, target score and winner.
• Look back at every round of every game.

RUMMY
• 2 or 3 players. Scorer shows how many cards to deal.
• Tap the cards of a meld to add its points live.
• At the end of the round, enter the points left in each hand. Scorer deducts them.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• No account. No ads. No tracking.
• Your games stay on your device.
• Works without internet. The TV link uses only your local Wi-Fi.

Scorer keeps the score. You play the game.
