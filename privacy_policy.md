# Privacy Policy

**App:** Scorer (`com.scoring_offline`)
**Effective date:** November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Scorer handles information. Scorer is an offline score keeper for card games. It works on your phone and, optionally, on a TV in the same local network.

## Summary

- Scorer does not collect personal information.
- Scorer has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics.
- Scorer does not send data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.
- Game data stays on your devices and your local network.

## Information the app uses

### Game data
- Player names, scores and round results that you enter.
- This data stays in the app on your device. The developer cannot see it.

### Settings stored on your device
- The name of the TV you last connected to.
- A TV address (IP and port) that you typed in manually, if any.
- This data is stored only on your phone, in private app storage.

## Network use

Scorer uses the network only inside your local network (Wi-Fi):

- **Phone to TV link:** The phone finds a TV running Scorer with local network discovery (mDNS/NSD). The phone then sends the current scoreboard to the TV over a direct local connection.
- **App sharing:** You can choose to share the app's installation file (APK) with a TV. The phone serves the file over the local network to the device that requests it.
- **Google Cast:** If you cast the scoreboard to a Chromecast or Cast-enabled TV, the app uses Google Cast. The scoreboard image is streamed from your phone over the local network. Google Cast is provided by Google, and Google's privacy policy applies to it: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app requests these Android permissions for the features above:

- `INTERNET` — open local network connections.
- `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` and `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` — check the Wi-Fi connection and local address.
- `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE` — find TVs on the local network.

## Sharing of information

- The developer does not sell, rent or share any information.
- The developer does not receive any information from the app.

## Backup

Android can include the app's local data in your device backup, if you turned on backup in your device settings. Google's backup service handles this data under your Google account settings.

## Children

Scorer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data deletion

- To delete all app data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
